Services

01 · PRODUCT ENGINEERING

Product development

I develop mechanical products end to end, from concept and solution architecture through design and validation to preparation for series production. I design with functional requirements, manufacturing technology, cost, reliability and scalability in mind.

02 · PROTOTYPING AND NPI

From concept to launch

I accelerate the path from concept to finished product through rapid prototyping and an iterative development process. I verify technical solutions, eliminate issues early, and prepare both the product and the process for launch and series production.

03 · ADDITIVE MANUFACTURING

Additive manufacturing

I use additive manufacturing for rapid prototyping, design validation, and the production of functional components and tooling. I select the right solution for each project by optimising geometry, material, process parameters and cost.

04 · CONSULTING

Engineering and manufacturing consulting

I support companies in technical and manufacturing decisions, from product-design assessment to analysis of the complete manufacturing process. I identify bottlenecks, risks and optimisation opportunities, providing recommendations grounded in technical analysis and manufacturing experience.
