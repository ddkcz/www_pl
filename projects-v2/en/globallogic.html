Challenge

Integrate precision mechanics, electromechanics and validation

Role and work

From mechanism design to documented validation

The scope covered subsystem design, component integration, development of prototyping and testing facilities, and product-documentation management.

01 · DESIGN

Precision mechanisms and integration

  • Precision mechanisms and mechanical and electromechanical subsystems for a surgical robot arm.
  • Plastic, sheet-metal, cast and machined components developed according to DFM/DFA and production requirements.
  • Selection and integration of COTS components, drives, sensors, wiring and mechatronic elements into complete assemblies.
02 · PROTOTYPE AND TESTS

Iterations driven by evidence

  • Leading prototyping, performing design analyses and iteratively improving designs based on test results.
  • Planning and conducting mechanical and functional tests.
  • Defining acceptance criteria and preparing test documentation.
  • Designing fixtures and test rigs supporting product development and validation.
03 · LABORATORY

Facilities for rapid development

  • Building an electromechanical laboratory for rapid prototyping, 3D printing and precision-mechanism assembly.
  • Establishing the capability for mechanical, vision-system and robotic testing.
  • Using a robotic arm to verify vision-system performance and the integration of mechanics with automation.
04 · QUALITY AND COOPERATION

Documentation and medical-device processes

  • Product-documentation management in Arena PLM, including EBOM structures and ECO/ECR processes.
  • Cooperation with mechanical, electrical, software and vision-system teams in an Agile environment.
  • Work in accordance with medical-device development processes and IEC 60601-1.
Facts and outcomes

Scope delivered within the project

The project went beyond models and mechanisms to include physical prototyping facilities, documented testing, and management of product structures and engineering changes.
