SHEET 03 / 05 · SERVICES

Services

My expertise includes product development, R&D projects, prototyping, manufacturing launches, process optimisation, and the organisation and development of production. I build and develop teams, support the creation of R&D structures, and help companies progress from an initial idea to a repeatable and efficient manufacturing process.

OFFER

Choose the right engagement format

01 · PRODUCT REVIEW

Product Review

An independent assessment of a concept, prototype or established product. I clarify requirements, identify risks and find optimisation opportunities before the next investment decision.

Choose this when

Before prototyping, design freeze or manufacturing — or when an existing product needs lower cost, simpler assembly, greater reliability or preparation for scaling.

The scope may include
  • requirements, functions and acceptance criteria;
  • design, tolerances, materials, interfaces and DFMA;
  • optimisation of cost, assembly, testing and service.
02 · PRODUCT DEVELOPMENT

Product development through launch

I lead the project from requirements and architecture through design, prototypes and testing to documentation and manufacturing launch.

Choose this when

You have an idea or early prototype and need to structure the stages, technical decisions and transition to manufacturing.

The scope may include
  • project stages, priorities and technical decisions;
  • design, prototypes, testing and subsequent revisions;
  • suppliers, documentation and launch preparation.
03 · FRACTIONAL LEAD

Fractional Product Engineer / R&D Lead

Ongoing product-development support: technical decisions, team and supplier coordination, and the connection between R&D and manufacturing.

Choose this when

The project needs continuity and a technical owner, but not yet a complete R&D department.

The scope may include
  • development plan, priorities and design reviews;
  • coordination of the team, suppliers, testing and changes;
  • development of the R&D process and its connection with manufacturing.
04 · ENGINEERING SUPPORT

Technical problem resolution

Focused support with a design or manufacturing problem: from root-cause analysis to verification of the solution.

Choose this when

A failure, unsuccessful test, or an assembly, quality or cost issue is blocking the next project stage.

The scope may include
  • evidence gathering and problem reproduction;
  • root-cause analysis and solution options;
  • test plan, decision criteria and implementation support.
