PRJ-007 Cedrowa Stolarnia 2024 — 2026

Cedrowa Stolarnia

Role: Owner

It all began while I was building my own house. By making the custom furniture myself, I saved approximately PLN 40,000 compared with the lowest quotation, followed by another PLN 25,000 on the staircase — the final material cost was around PLN 700, plus several drawer slides and timber left over from construction. Before I had even finished the house, I decided to establish a cabinetmaking business.


For more than a year I employed two full-time cabinetmakers; around five people worked within the team in total, and revenue reached PLN 450–500k. I built relationships with 5–8 wholesalers and equipped the workshop from scratch, with a total investment of approximately PLN 80–100k.

What I am particularly proud of

I am most proud that I had the courage to build a company from scratch and take full responsibility for its results. I created the entire business — from organising the workshop and team, through processes, procedures and the supplier network, to sales and project delivery. At the same time, I looked for ways to scale and automate the work. This resulted in a custom JavaScript application that generated a complete bill of materials from a 3D model, reducing estimating and ordering from hours to seconds. Above all, I value the decision to try: entering an unfamiliar field, building something with my own hands, making mistakes, learning from them and turning the project into a real, profitable business.

Completed projects

  • Custom furniture for five apartments in Kraków — 80, 65, 55, 40 and 35 m²
  • Four custom kitchens
  • Furniture for two kitchens and ten hotel rooms in Limanowa

Lessons learned

The company was profitable, but it was not the direction I wanted to pursue long term. The realities of the Polish construction industry involved practices I was unwilling to accept, so I decided to close the company and return fully to engineering. The business paid for itself and earned good money, but above all it taught me an enormous amount — both about running a company and about myself.
