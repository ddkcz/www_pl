PRJ-005 ABB E-mobility 2021 — 2022

ABB Formula E — a charger built for racing

Role: Senior Mechanical Engineer

Electric-vehicle charging systems (ABB E-mobility / Formula E)

I contributed to the development of electric-vehicle charging systems, including infrastructure used in Formula E racing. I was responsible for mechanical-component design, support during new-product introduction and design optimisation for series production. My work covered the complete product-development cycle — from concept and 3D design through prototyping and testing to production readiness.

Responsibilities included:

  • Designing mechanical components for AC wallboxes, 150 kW DC fast chargers and power-distribution units (Contactor Box).
  • Designing busbars and power-cable routing with electrical, assembly and service requirements in mind.
  • Developing mounting components and internal structures for easy assembly, high reliability and safe operation.
  • Optimising designs according to DFMA principles to simplify production and assembly and reduce cost.
  • Participating in NPI, preparing new products for manufacturing launch and cooperating with production and suppliers.
  • Working with mechanical, electrical and manufacturing teams to resolve design issues and implement changes.
  • Creating and implementing design changes and preparing technical documentation for production.
  • Planning change implementation within the mechanical team and improving work organisation so several designers could work concurrently on one CAD model and deliver the product on time.
  • Participating in prototype assembly, testing and validation before series production.
