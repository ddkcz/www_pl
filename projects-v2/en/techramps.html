21st-century sports facilities

Developing new product lines, leading an international investment project, and automating estimation and design for the sports and recreation industry.

Context and challenge

Combining product development, investment delivery and process improvement

I worked in the team responsible for developing products for the sports and recreation industry. My scope included designing new solutions and taking them from concept to implementation, standardising engineering work, and cooperating with sales and production.

Product development Project management Production Automation BOM DFMA
01 · FlowParks

Development of new product lines

I was responsible for developing solutions combining steel structures and timber elements, considering durability, aesthetics and manufacturing capability.

01 · PRODUCT

Larix and FlowPark Home lines

  • Designing the complete Larix product line, including its concept, product architecture and naming.
  • Managing the development of new product lines, including FlowParks Larix and FlowPark Home.
  • Developing a universal design template that enabled existing products to be adapted to a new construction technology.
02 · IMPLEMENTATION

Process, documentation and production

  • Coordinating cooperation between design, sales and production departments.
  • Standardising technical documentation and design processes to improve the engineering team's work.
  • Supporting new-product launches and optimising solutions for series production.

Result: the project produced a complete product family used in installations across multiple international markets and significantly improved the process of designing new solutions.

02 · ROAM Further

End-to-end investment project management in the USA

I led the delivery of one of the largest parkour facilities in the United States, from the first client discussions through completion.

01 · DESIGN

Technical solution

  • Creating the functional design for an existing warehouse and adapting it into a modern sports facility.
  • Structural design and coordination of technical documentation.
  • Preparing cost estimates and optimising investment costs.
02 · DELIVERY

Client, team and logistics

  • Leading the project end to end — from initial client contact to handover of the finished facility.
  • Negotiating contract terms and maintaining ongoing client communication.
  • Managing the project schedule and coordinating a four-person design team.
  • Organising production, packing and preparation for international transport.
  • Working with contractors and stakeholders throughout delivery.

Result: the project combined design, management, production organisation and client-facing responsibilities within one international delivery.

03 · Estimation and production

Automating quotation preparation and BOM generation

I designed a tool that automated project estimates and generated preliminary material documentation for pump tracks.

01 · DATA

The 3D model as a source of information

  • Integrating data from 3D models created in SketchUp.
  • Including parameters such as surface type, installation method and project location.
  • Automatically generating preliminary bills of materials.
02 · PROCESS

One source for three departments

  • Developing a calculator that automated commercial quotation preparation.
  • Significantly reducing estimate preparation time and errors caused by manual calculations.
  • Improving cooperation between sales, design and production through a single source of data.

Result: the automation improved organisational efficiency, shortened quotation lead time and reduced errors caused by manual calculations.

04 · WakePro

Automating wakeboard-obstacle design

For my degree thesis, completed in cooperation with WakePro, I developed a complete process supporting wakeboard-obstacle design.

01 · ANALYSIS

Stability and structural parameters

  • Analysing the stability of floating objects as a function of their geometry.
  • Using engineering tools to analyse structural parameters and functional properties.
02 · METHODOLOGY

A repeatable design process

  • Developing a methodology for designing new wakeboard obstacles from scratch.
  • Building an automated design process supporting mechanical designers.
  • Developing solutions that enabled new product variants to be created faster.

Result: the project combined mechanics, product design and engineering-process automation, establishing the foundations of a modern product-development process.
