Formula Student suspension design

At AGH Racing, a student engineering team at AGH UST, I was responsible for several complex mechanical projects involving suspension systems. I also worked on design processes intended to improve team efficiency. I participated in building two combustion-powered race cars and Poland's first electric Formula Student car.

Engineering projects

  • Steel control-arm design for two Grażyna race cars.
  • Steering-column design for the combustion-powered Grażyna.
  • Composite control arms for the electric LEM — a 53% reduction in total control-arm mass.
  • Manufacturing components on a CNC milling machine as part of the team's work.

Team achievements

  • Formula SAE Australasia 2020 — 1st place in Design.
  • Formula SAE Michigan 2019 — 3rd in Acceleration, 15th overall out of 139 teams.
  • Formula FS Netherlands 2018 — 1st in Acceleration, 2nd in Skid Pad, 2nd in Autocross and 5th overall.
  • Formula FS Spain 2018 — 1st place in Acceleration.
  • Formula SAE Michigan 2018 — 4th in Business Plan, 5th in Endurance, 6th in Skid Pad and 9th overall out of 118 teams.

Scientific publications

  • Engineering thesis: “Structural analysis of a Formula Student suspension control-arm joint in ANSYS”
  • Analysis of lap joints in the design of a composite drive shaft — ISBN: 978-83-949477-4-3
  • Formula Student suspension-component design using a steering knuckle as an example — ISBN: 978-83-949477-2-9
  • Design of carbon-fibre Formula Student control arms — ISBN: 978-83-949477-2-9
