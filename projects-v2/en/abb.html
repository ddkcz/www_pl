Challenge

Prepare the mechanics of power systems for assembly, service and series production

The design had to account for electrical, assembly and service requirements. The scope combined component, busbar and power-cable routing design with DFMA, documentation, engineering changes, and cooperation with production and suppliers.

Role and work

Four connected areas of responsibility

The scope progressed from mechanical design and electrical integration to preparing both the product and the design team for changes and production.

01 · INTEGRATION

Charging-system mechanics

  • Mechanical components for AC wallboxes, 150 kW DC fast chargers and Contactor Box power-distribution units.
  • Busbars and power-cable routing designed around electrical, assembly and service requirements.
  • Mounting components and internal structures supporting easy assembly, high reliability and safe operation.
02 · DFMA AND NPI

Production readiness

  • Design optimisation according to DFMA principles to simplify production and assembly and reduce cost.
  • Participation in NPI and preparation of new products for manufacturing launch.
  • Cooperation with production and suppliers while preparing solutions for series manufacture.
03 · CHANGES AND DOCUMENTATION

Mechanical-team coordination

  • Cooperation with mechanical, electrical and manufacturing teams to resolve design issues and implement changes.
  • Creation and implementation of design changes and technical documentation supporting production.
  • Planning change implementation within the mechanical team and organising concurrent work by several designers on one CAD model.
04 · PROTOTYPE AND TESTS

Validation before series production

  • Participation in prototype assembly and testing of the designed solutions.
  • Design validation before the start of series production.
  • Support throughout the complete cycle from concept to production readiness.
