ASENSUS Surgical — surgical robot

Role: Senior Mechanical Engineer / Product Lead

Development of a next-generation surgical robot

I was responsible for the mechanical and electromechanical development of surgical-robot subsystems, participating in the entire product-development process — from concept through design and prototyping to testing and validation in a medical-device environment.


Scope of work:

  • Designing precision mechanisms and mechanical and electromechanical subsystems for a surgical robot arm.
  • Developing plastic, sheet-metal, cast and machined components according to DFM/DFA principles and production requirements.
  • Selecting and integrating COTS components, drives, sensors, wiring and other mechatronic elements into complete assemblies.
  • Leading prototyping, performing design analyses and iteratively improving designs based on test results.
  • Planning and conducting mechanical and functional tests, defining acceptance criteria and preparing test documentation.
  • Designing fixtures and test rigs supporting product development and validation.
  • Building an electromechanical laboratory for rapid prototyping, 3D printing, precision-mechanism assembly and mechanical, vision-system and robotic testing.
  • Running tests with a robotic arm to verify vision-system performance and the integration of mechanics with automation.
  • Managing product documentation in Arena PLM, creating EBOM structures and supporting ECO/ECR processes.
  • Working with mechanical, electrical, software and vision-system teams in an Agile environment.
  • Working in accordance with medical-device development processes and IEC 60601-1, with emphasis on safety, reliability and quality.

The project gave me experience in medical-device design, where solution validation, technical documentation and close cooperation within an interdisciplinary team were as important as mechanical-design expertise.
