Designing and manufacturing Formula Student suspension components, from steel control arms to a composite solution for an electric race car.

RoleSuspension Engineer
Context and challenge

Suspension designed for real competition

At AGH Racing, a student engineering team at AGH UST, I was responsible for several complex mechanical projects involving suspension systems. I also worked on design processes intended to improve team efficiency.


I participated in building two combustion-powered race cars and Poland's first electric Formula Student car.

Role and activities

From concept to manufactured component

The scope combined component design, structural analysis, composite design development and hands-on involvement in manufacturing parts.

01 · STEEL

Control arms

Steel control-arm design for two Grażyna race cars.

02 · SYSTEM

Steering

Steering-column design for the combustion-powered Grażyna.

03 · CFRP

Composites

Composite control-arm design for the electric LEM.

04 · CNC

Manufacturing

Manufacturing components on a CNC milling machine as part of the team's work.

Facts and results

Engineering outcome and team effort

The result of my own engineering work is shown separately from the achievements delivered collectively by AGH Racing Team.

3 scientific publications connected with my work in the team
