Context and challenge

From a blank sheet to a competition-ready car

The project began with a friend while we were working at AGH Racing. We had no ready-made solution or specialist team to turn to, so we had to learn, design, manufacture and verify most things ourselves.


A Fiat Seicento Sporting served as our learning platform for several years. The next stage was an Alfa Romeo MiTo, the first car in Poland that we prepared for competition to RallyN specification.

Role and activities

Design verified directly in the car

Solutions followed the full path from concept, selection, design and construction through testing, competition and subsequent upgrades.

01 · LEARNING

Construction and development of an amateur rally car from 2019 to 2023.

02 · RALLYN

Alfa Romeo MiTo

Construction of a MiTo QV to RallyN specification since 2023.

03 · ITERATIONS

Development

Continuous vehicle upgrades and adaptation to rally requirements.

04 · COMPETITION

Co-driver

Competing in amateur SKJS rallies from 2019 to 2023, followed by the Pro2S / RallyN class.

Working method

Accountability for decisions and immediate feedback

Every solution had to work not only on a computer screen but above all in the car during competition, where the margin for error is small and the consequences of a poor decision appear immediately.


Experimenting, testing, learning and continuously improving shaped the way I work, which I apply to engineering and manufacturing projects today.
